## What to build

Stable project/parent relationships support a readable fullscreen office with all observed main agents and subagents visible, pet details on demand and overflow beyond four projects.

PetsTown observes existing Codex agents. Fullscreen presentation does not control their execution; genuine ChatGPT/Codex pets are required.

## Acceptance criteria — accepted fullscreen scope

- [x] One fullscreen open-plan office retains stable project/agent allocations without requiring project labels or a roster.
- [x] Main agents and subagents render by default; no global hiding toggle or expandable group controls are required.
- [x] Visible pets retain request attention and can be selected for available details.
- [x] Layout supports more than four projects; the background's four desk clusters are not a cap.
- [x] Joining/leaving agents and unknown parents do not duplicate or orphan pets.
