# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Codex users and hackathon teammates observing their existing working agents.

## Product Purpose

Show genuine Codex pets in one shared office. The office observes work; it does not launch or control agents. See design.md and docs/contracts/office-v1.md for the authoritative behavior.

## Brand Personality

Playful, calm, companionable. Preserve the established native pet artwork and restrained scene controls.

## Anti-references

Generated replacement pet characters, invented work updates, and agent orchestration disguised as visual controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep observed work truthful.
- Keep genuine pets recognizable.
- Make the room explorable without hiding attention or selection.
- Keep scene presentation separate from agent execution.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard selection, readable status text and reduced-motion support. Retain the 2D scene if WebGL is unavailable.
